Basin-Scale Coupled Poromechanics

A MOOSE-based software pipeline for two-way coupled flow and deformation across geological models.

  • MOOSE
  • PorousFlow
  • Python
  • MPI
  • Verification

I am building a MOOSE-based scientific software pipeline for two-way coupled poromechanics at field and basin scales. The framework, called JAVELINA, transforms geological surfaces, spatial property data, well trajectories, and operating schedules into reproducible PorousFlow simulations while keeping coordinate transforms, material assignment, source allocation, completions, and boundary conditions auditable.

JAVELINA supports serial and MPI execution and has been exercised against six analytical, semi-analytical, and manufactured verification cases. Its pipeline connects geological preprocessing, simulation, and post-processing without hiding the assumptions between them.

I developed DC-ISM to connect AI workload states and hardware fleets to the facility and grid questions that energy planners need to answer. The Python engine decomposes hourly demand into IT, cooling, electrical, network, storage, and house loads, with PUE and water use emerging from the selected hardware, cooling architecture, redundancy, weather, and operating profile.

The broader workflow supports first-principles FLOPs accounting, coarse training-time estimation, measured-weather ingestion, tariffs and carbon accounting, sensitivity screening, correlated uncertainty, scenario-year analysis, supply-land screening, and risk-aware comparison across sites and designs. It exports a serializable DemandCase for supply-side tools. A 155-test suite, typed interfaces, explicit scope boundaries, and a limitations register keep assumptions and unsupported effects auditable.

Utah FORGE Stimulation Analysis

Second place, GEODE Engineering Datathon — multi-modal diagnostics for an enhanced geothermal system.

  • Python
  • DTS
  • Microseismicity
  • Tracer analysis
  • EGS

Over one weekend, my teammate and I integrated well surveys, stimulation records, distributed fiber-optic measurements, tracer recovery, and microseismic events. I wrote the analysis code shown in our presentation and used the independent data streams to interpret stage-level connectivity and preferential flow paths.

We proposed a targeted restimulation program using perturbed-stress analysis, oriented perforations, isolation or diversion, and pulsed initiation. The inferred fracture geometries and stress-shadow explanation remain interpretations rather than ground truth.
