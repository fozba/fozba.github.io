Nevada Play Fairway Analysis

2nd Place | GEODE Geoscience Datathon. Deep learning for blind geothermal exploration.

PyTorchArcGISCNNGIS rasterizationgeothermal

For the GEODE Datathon, I evaluated the Nevada Play Fairway dataset, an integrated synthesis of geological, geochemical, and geophysical signals used to identify blind geothermal systems.

I took a novel approach by reframing traditional spatial regression as a computer vision problem. After processing and rasterizing spatial features such as geodetic strain and Euclidean distance to faults in ArcGIS, I built a pixel-wise CNN architecture in PyTorch to predict overall site favorability.

By treating the stacked geospatial layers as a multi-channel image tensor, the model captured the non-linear, spatial relationships between faults and heat anomalies. Predictions agreed strongly with known training locations, validating the model's physical accuracy.

The real value, however, came from extrapolation. Examining the difference map, the network identified highly prospective new zones (the darker reds in the difference map) that standard linear methods undervalued; these zones remain geologically promising due to their proximity to local faults and permeability corridors.
