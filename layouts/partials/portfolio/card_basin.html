Integrated Basin Analysis

Building an open, basin-wide stratigraphic model of the Permian from public records.

PythonTexas RRC datastratigraphic modelingPlotly

Working in the Delaware Basin during my PhD, I noticed a gap: there is no openly available, basin-wide formation model for the Delaware or the broader Permian. Existing work almost universally relies on proprietary databases like Enverus, creating a barrier for geoscientists who need a reproducible baseline. To address this, I am independently building one from scratch using the Texas RRC database, extracting observed formation tops directly from public records. Initial results show that open-access data captures formation architecture remarkably well, closely matching existing cross-sections. I am now incorporating measurement uncertainty and integrating open-access well logs, ultimately producing a benchmark stratigraphic model.
