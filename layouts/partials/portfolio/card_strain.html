Ground Uplift Modeling

Connecting wastewater injection, pore pressure, and surface deformation to constrain induced seismicity risk.

numerical modelingInSARporoelasticityPython

Widespread wastewater injection elevates pore pressure across the Delaware Basin, producing measurable ground uplift confirmed by satellite measurements. To understand the chain from injection to deformation, I build and validate numerical models that capture the underlying physics. I am extending this framework to quantify the pore pressure increase and its contribution to induced earthquakes.
