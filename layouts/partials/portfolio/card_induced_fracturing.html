Wastewater Injection-Induced Fracturing in the Delaware Basin

Validating 3D coupled-process models to define safe injection envelopes for active SWD wells.

ResFracOpenFOAMPythonwell logsporomechanics

I develop comprehensive, coupled-process geomechanical models of the Delaware Basin to investigate the risks of wastewater injection-induced fracturing and pore pressure elevation. I build and simulate these models in advanced fractured reservoir simulators (ResFrac, OpenFOAM), integrating diverse datasets including well logs, public injection records, and regional structural models.
