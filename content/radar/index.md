Welcome to my Radar page! Here I keep a list of inspiring individuals, interesting YouTube channels, innovative companies, and other resources I find valuable or worth following.

## People

### Ilya Sutksever
Ilya is a computer scientist who works on machine intelligence. What separates him from many others in the area is his vision about machine intelligence and [AGI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_general_intelligence), and him being an exceptional scientist in the field. He was OpenAI's former Chief Scientist, but left the company and started his own company, [SSI](https://ssi.inc/), which entirely focuses on building _super-intelligent machine_. So this guy is not after building AI for profit, he is after solving the intelligence itself. I really recommend to keep an eye on his work just in case he builds the next step in evolution.

### George Hotz
If one person deserves the title "hacker", that would be George Hotz, aka _geohot_. This guy started his career as a cybersecurity hacker, and these days he is doing 8-hour long Twitch streams of building an entire [autograd](https://tinygrad.org/) framework that aims to give computing power to the ordinary people. Not only on software side, he is also literally doing hardware engineering with the existing GPUs of almost all major GPU-producing companies like NVIDIA, Intel and AMD. He is an incredibly talented guy, definitely worth checking, with an incredible motto and endeavor: **PetaFLOPs to the People**
