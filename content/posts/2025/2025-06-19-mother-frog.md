<p style="font-family: 'Georgia', serif;">
I was walking down the road after a hard rain in Houston. Just next to the curb, I noticed a low spot where rainwater had formed a puddle. In the puddle, I observed tens, or perhaps hundreds, of frog larvae. Apparently, when it rains in Houston, frogs roam everywhere. It must have been during such a time that a mother frog laid her eggs here.<br><br>
I thought to myself:<br><br>
"Poor babies, they have no chance at all."<br><br>
I kept thinking... Poor mother frog... She came here, sat in this puddle, thinking it was the right time and the right place. She gave her only shot in this life, perhaps, for something that was never going to be successful.<br><br>
I kept thinking... "How can the mother frog be this stupid? Laying her eggs in this tiny puddle of water that could never sustain her babies? Didn't she realize this water was meant to dry out? Didn't she know that it was meant to be gone? Something that can never be permanent. Didn't she notice that this place didn't have its own water, and she was at the mercy of occasional rains?"<br><br>
When I finished thinking, I felt mostly pity in my heart for the mother frog, for she tried her best in a lost cause. And perhaps, a bit of anger for her foolishness, since she couldn't tell the wrong place from the right one.
</p>
